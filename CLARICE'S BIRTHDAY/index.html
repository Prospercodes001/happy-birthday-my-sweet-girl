A LETTER FOR YOU ❤️

C

Happy 19th birthday, Ogechi Clarice Nzeadibe. ❤️

Honestly, I don't even know where to begin without making this unnecessarily sentimental 😂. But then again, I suppose you deserve a little sentimentality today, so I'll allow myself.

First of all, happy birthday, my sweet girl. ❤️

I want to say thank you. Thank you for the past 3/4 years. For everything, really. The beautiful moments, the difficult ones, the fights, the love, the truths, the lies, the laughter, the tears, the misunderstandings, the random conversations, the silly arguments and all the little things that somehow became memories I'll probably carry for a very long time.

Sometimes I genuinely sit back and wonder how two people who spent about 5/6 years barely being part of each other's lives somehow found their way back to one another. And not just found their way back, but somehow managed to create an entire history together. 😂

I still find it funny that you were literally the first person I spoke to when I walked into Mr. Kunle's JAMB class. Who would have thought that something as ordinary as attending lessons would eventually lead to all of this?

Then there was that period during lessons when there was obviously something going on between us, even if neither of us was particularly brave enough to admit it properly. 😂

And then, before that public holiday, I finally gathered enough courage to approach you properly.

The first kiss came after the public holiday.

I still remember that. I remember how things seemed to shift from that moment. How something that had previously existed in little glances, conversations and unspoken feelings suddenly became something real.

Then the lessons ended, and we were both practically wondering what would happen next. We genuinely thought not seeing each other anymore might be the end of whatever was beginning between us. But somehow, we kept it going for another month without even seeing each other.

And then came our first proper reunion, our first proper make-out and all the butterflies, excitement and other things that came with being two young people who clearly liked each other a little too much. 😂

And of course, how could I possibly forget our first pregnancy scare? 😭😂

I don't think either of us had ever been that terrified. We were practically imagining our entire lives changing overnight. Looking back, I sometimes laugh at how much we thought we understood about life when we were still figuring out so many things.

But those memories are ours. And regardless of how things eventually turned out, there's something rather beautiful about having experienced them with you.

Then came that period when I zoned out and practically left you hanging because of everything happening with me and my family at the time.

And honestly, looking back, I can imagine the number of things that must have crossed your mind. You probably questioned where my attention had gone, whether there was someone else, whether I had lost interest or whether there was something I wasn't telling you. I wouldn't even blame you if those thoughts crossed your mind because, from your end, all you probably saw was someone who had suddenly become distant without giving you much of an explanation.

But I wish you knew that, regardless of how things might have appeared, there was never someone else. And honestly, the idea of me looking elsewhere? 😂 Please. You were already giving me enough to think about. What exactly was I looking for that I hadn't already found in you? No girl came close to you in my eyes, and I genuinely wasn't interested in finding out whether anyone could.

You meant so much to me then. More than I probably knew how to express, and certainly more than my actions during that period suggested. You were genuinely one of the most important people in my life. I valued you immensely, your presence mattered to me, and having you in my corner meant more than you probably realised.

I wasn't taking your place in my life for granted, even though my inability to communicate might have made it seem that way.

I know I could have handled things differently. I could have let you in, explained what was happening and spared you some of the uncertainty. Instead, I withdrew, and I understand that my silence might have created its own hurt.

Eventually, I reached out again around the time you were preparing to go to school, and somehow, despite everything, we found our way back to each other. Looking back, there's something quite remarkable about that. After all the silence, unanswered questions and uncertainty, we somehow found ourselves talking again, giving what we had another opportunity to breathe.

But things weren't quite the same anymore.

You started pulling away, I did too, in my own ways. We had our arguments, misunderstandings, disagreements, blocking, unblocking, words we shouldn't have said and conversations we probably should have had but never did.

Sometimes I think we both wanted to be understood so badly that we forgot to understand each other.

And somehow, two people who genuinely cared about each other became capable of hurting each other in ways neither probably intended at the beginning.

I've reflected on that quite a bit. I won't sit here and paint myself as the innocent one because I certainly wasn't. There were things you did that hurt me, and there were things I did that hurt you. There were moments I could have been more patient, more understanding and perhaps a little less stubborn. 😂

But I also think we were young. We were learning about love, emotions, expectations, communication and ourselves. Sometimes we got it right, sometimes we got it terribly wrong, and sometimes we were just two stubborn individuals who probably needed to calm down and listen to each other. 😂

Still, I wouldn't call those years a waste.

Because whatever name we eventually give what we had, it meant something to me. You meant something to me. And I don't think the fact that things didn't work out exactly as we imagined means we should suddenly pretend that none of it mattered.

A lot has happened since those days, and I must admit, life has been quite an interesting journey.

Between navigating UNILAG, discovering new opportunities, getting involved in different projects, travelling, meeting different people, exploring new environments and gradually finding my feet in the things I want to build, I've come to realise that there's so much more to life than the little world we once knew.

There are places I haven't seen, experiences I haven't had, people I haven't met, dreams I haven't pursued and versions of myself I haven't even discovered yet.

And honestly, that's exciting.

I'm beginning to appreciate the beauty of growth, of having something to look forward to, of being intentional about my future and of allowing life to unfold without feeling the need to have every single thing figured out.

I've also learnt that not every meaningful connection has to be forced into a particular definition or outcome. Sometimes people meet at a particular point in their lives, leave impressions on each other and continue growing in different directions. Sometimes they find their way back. And sometimes life surprises them in ways they never anticipated.

I suppose that's part of growing up.

And yeah, sometimes I still think about you. 😂

Not necessarily because I'm sitting around reminiscing or anything, but because some memories have a funny way of finding their way back to you. Sometimes it's something you said, a particular moment we shared or just some random thing that reminds me of your existence. And before I know it, I'm smiling to myself. I suppose that's what happens when someone has been part of your life for as long as you were part of mine.

And no, don't let that get to your head. You were troublesome enough already. 😂❤️

I don't have to pretend you've become just another person I once knew. You were someone I shared a significant part of my growing years with. Someone whose presence, personality, affection and even occasional ability to get on my nerves became part of my everyday life for a while.

And I won't lie, there were things about you that I really liked. Your little mannerisms, your sass, your stubbornness, the way you could be sweet one minute and an absolute handful the next. 😂

You certainly weren't the easiest person to deal with, but then again, I doubt I was exactly a walk in the park either.

And perhaps that's what made some of our moments so interesting. We had our own little world, our own language, our own jokes and those random moments that probably wouldn't make sense to anybody else.

I don't think you're perfect, and I'm certainly not claiming to be. But there was something about us that I found genuinely special. Something that, even with all its imperfections, I wouldn't casually dismiss.

And I want you to know that my appreciation for you isn't dependent on whether we eventually become something again or remain two people who occasionally check in on each other.

If we're friends, that's fine. If life takes us in completely different directions, that's fine too. And if somewhere down the line our paths cross again and we discover that there's still a little something worth exploring, well, I suppose we'll see. 😂

I'm not making promises on behalf of the future, and I'm certainly not asking you to make any either.

I'm simply comfortable acknowledging that some people leave a particular kind of impression, and you happen to be one of those people.

And about that whole different-worlds conversation you once had with me. 😂

I still remember you telling me we're from different worlds. You at Pan-Atlantic, me at UNILAG. The distance, the financial differences, the different lifestyles and everything else you felt made things complicated.

I understand that conversation a lot better now than I probably did then.

Life comes with responsibilities, different circumstances, different priorities and realities that sometimes aren't immediately obvious when all you're thinking about is how much you enjoy someone's company.

But I've also come to understand that people are constantly growing. Circumstances change, perspectives evolve, opportunities come and go, and the people we are today won't necessarily be the people we become tomorrow.

I'm not suggesting that everything suddenly becomes simple because of that. I'm just saying that life is a lot bigger than the assumptions we sometimes make about where people belong or what is possible.

And besides, I don't think two people need to have identical lives to genuinely appreciate one another.

I guess what I'm trying to say is that I don't think two people have to be in each other's lives in exactly the same way they once were to still have a genuine appreciation for one another. Sometimes it's a random conversation, a little banter, checking up on each other or sharing something that makes you think the other person would find it funny. Nothing complicated, no unnecessary expectations. Just two people who know each other beyond the usual pleasantries and can still share a laugh every now and then.

And honestly, I'd rather we never get to a point where we feel the need to act like complete strangers who once knew everything about each other.

Anyway, enough of my philosophical birthday speech. Before you start thinking I've suddenly become a relationship counsellor. 😭😂

Today is about you.

You're turning 19!

NINETEEN.

I hope you appreciate how quickly you're growing because it feels like just yesterday we were two young people at JAMB lessons trying to figure out what we were doing with ourselves. And now look at you.

Another year older, another year of experiences, another year of becoming.

I genuinely want this new year of your life to be a good one. I hope you have more reasons to laugh, more moments that make you excited about waking up, more wins to celebrate and fewer things to worry about. I hope the things you've been praying and quietly working towards begin to fall into place. And when things don't go exactly as planned, I hope you find the strength and grace to keep going.

I hope 19 treats you kindly, Clarice. I hope you get to experience so many beautiful things, make memories you'll be telling stories about years from now and find yourself in places you once only imagined. You deserve to enjoy life, not just work towards a future version of it.

And knowing you, I'm sure there are already about a hundred things on your wishlist. 😂❤️

I hope you become more confident in yourself, more intentional about your dreams and more comfortable with the beautiful woman you're becoming.

I hope you never have to shrink yourself to fit into places you're meant to outgrow. I hope you never settle for less than the respect, love and consideration you deserve.

I hope you meet people who see beyond your beauty, appreciate your mind, respect your individuality and genuinely care about your happiness.

And please, don't let that your stubbornness become a permanent personality trait. There is still room for improvement. 😂

But seriously, I hope you never lose the little things that make you Ogechi. Your personality, your laughter, your confidence, your softness and even that slightly troublesome side of yours.

You deserve beautiful things, Clarice. And I sincerely hope life gives you plenty of reasons to experience them.

Thank you for being part of my story.

Thank you for the memories, the laughter, the affection, the lessons, the arguments, the unexpected moments and even the experiences we probably wouldn't willingly repeat. 😂

Thank you for being someone I once got to know in ways not everybody gets to.

I don't regret meeting you. I don't regret the beautiful moments we shared, and I don't think I'd erase our story just because it had its difficult chapters.

Perhaps we didn't have everything figured out. Perhaps we weren't always what the other person needed. Perhaps timing, circumstances and our own individual shortcomings got in the way.

But we lived it. And for what it's worth, I'm grateful we did.

So here's to 19. 🥂❤️

Here's to growth, beautiful memories, new adventures, bigger dreams, unexpected blessings, genuine love, healing and becoming everything we're capable of becoming.

Here's to a life that keeps surprising you in the most beautiful ways.

And who knows? Perhaps a few years from now, we'll sit somewhere, look back at all this and laugh at how dramatic, stubborn and ridiculously young we were. 😂

Until then, keep being you. Keep growing. Keep shining. Keep giving life reasons to be interesting.

And never forget that regardless of how life unfolds, there will always be someone who genuinely wishes you well and is happy to see you win.

Happy 19th birthday, Ogechi Clarice Nzeadibe.

Enjoy your day, pretty girl. You deserve to be celebrated. ❤️
